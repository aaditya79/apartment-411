*** FICTIONAL SAMPLE LEASE: FOR DEMONSTRATING APARTMENT 411 ONLY ***
This is not a real lease. The landlord, tenant and terms below are invented, and
some clauses are deliberately problematic so the review has something to find.

RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease is made on October 1, 2026 between Claremont Gardens Realty LLC
("Landlord") and Jordan Example ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES. Landlord leases to Tenant Apartment 4B at 184 Claremont Avenue,
New York, NY 10027 (the "Apartment").

2. TERM. The term of this Lease is twelve (12) months, beginning on November 1, 2026
and ending on October 31, 2027.

3. RENT. Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $2,850.00, due in advance on the first (1st)
day of each month.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Upon signing, Tenant shall deposit $5,700.00, equal to two (2)
months' rent, as security for Tenant's performance of this Lease. Landlord will return
the deposit, less lawful deductions, within fourteen (14) days after Tenant moves out.

5. LATE CHARGES. If any rent is not received more than two (2) days after it is due,
Tenant shall pay a late fee of $75.00.

6. APPLICATION FEE. Tenant has paid a non-refundable application and credit check fee
of $150.00, which is not refundable under any circumstances.

7. HEAT AND HOT WATER. Heat and hot water are included in the rent. Landlord shall
provide heat and hot water as required by law.

8. ATTORNEY'S FEES. If Landlord retains an attorney to enforce any term of this Lease
or to recover possession of the Apartment, Tenant shall pay Landlord's reasonable
attorney's fees and costs.

9. REPAIRS. Tenant shall promptly notify Landlord in writing of any condition requiring
repair. Landlord shall make repairs within a reasonable time.

10. WINDOW GUARDS. Window guard notice: Landlord is required by law to install window
guards in the Apartment if a child 10 years of age or younger lives there, or if Tenant
requests them. Tenant must complete and return the annual window guard notice.

11. LEAD-BASED PAINT. Lead-based paint notice: this building was built before 1960.
Tenant acknowledges receipt of the Lease/Commencement of Occupancy Notice for
Prevention of Lead-Based Paint Hazards and the EPA pamphlet.

12. SPRINKLER DISCLOSURE. THERE IS NO MAINTAINED AND OPERATIVE SPRINKLER SYSTEM IN
THE LEASED PREMISES.

13. RIDER. In the event of any conflict, this rider controls. Monthly rent for the
Apartment is $2,950.00, payable on the first day of each month.

Landlord: Claremont Gardens Realty LLC, by its Managing Member

Tenant: Jordan Example
